You made it through the lock. Welcome to the back room, where the baristas keep the recipe they make for themselves after closing.

## The after-hours shot

- 18 g of a light, washed coffee, ground just fine enough to run for 30 seconds
- 45 g out: a longer ratio than the menu's 1:2, sweeter and more transparent
- Poured over a single large ice cube in a chilled glass
- A few drops of water from the steam wand's tip, just to open the aroma

It's an espresso that behaves like a pour-over: bright, sweet and gone in two sips.

## Why it was locked

It wasn't, really. This page exists to show that a Quartz garden can hide things. The page is encrypted at build time, left out of search, the graph and the explorer, and decrypted in your browser only once you know the password. A puzzle in front of it turns a security feature into a game.
