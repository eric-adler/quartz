---

A digital garden doesn't have to read like a stack of documents. These pages are experiments in reading: text that adapts to the reader, navigation that remembers where you've been, and visuals that run in the browser.

Each experiment is switched on per page, so the rest of the site stays ordinary. The supporting [[demo/coffee/index|coffee notes]] give the demos something real to link to.

## Reading that adapts

- [[choose-your-depth|Choose your own depth]]: an argument that checks in after each claim and adds support only where you ask for it.
- [[concept-links|Dashed-underline concepts]]: hover a term for its core idea, click to read the full note in a drawer.
- [[sidenotes|Sidenotes]]: footnotes in the margin, next to the sentence they belong to.
- [[telescopic-text|Telescopic text]]: sentences that unfold in place, phrase by phrase.

## Navigation that remembers

- [[t-profile|T-profile navigation]]: a deep rail through the page's sections and a wide bar across related notes, forming a T.
- [[personal-graph|Your garden]]: the garden's graph, sized and lit by your own visits.
- [[your-path|Your path]]: a map of the route you took through the garden.
- [[flashcards|Flashcards]]: any note's highlights become a quiz.
- [[puzzle-lock|The barista's lock]]: four clues spell the password to a hidden page.

## Later

- **Video annotations**: notes that appear in time with a YouTube video.
- **Scrollytelling**: a diagram that changes as you scroll.
- **Reactive document**: numbers in a sentence that you can drag.
- **Annotated case study**: comment pins on images and a before/after slider.
- **Read aloud**: a page that reads itself and highlights each word as it goes.
- **Shader showcase**, a **hover lab** of 25 shader link effects, **morphing page transitions** and a **semantic map** of the garden.
