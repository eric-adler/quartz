> [!info] Demo: your path through the garden
> Like a map app's location history, but for reading. The route draws itself from where you **started** (the green ring) to where **you are now** (the pulsing pin). Newer hops are bolder, and repeated hops curve apart so loops stay visible. Below the map, your visits are grouped into sessions with the time you spent on each page.

## Reading the map

Every note sits where it does on the [[personal-graph|personal graph]], so the two pages complement each other: the graph shows _how much_ you've read, the path shows _in what order_. Short hops between neighbours mean you were following links; long jumps across the map usually mean search, the explorer or the back button.

Your history stays in this browser. Only pages in the showcase are recorded.
