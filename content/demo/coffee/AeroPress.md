---
title: "AeroPress"
description: "A plastic syringe that combines immersion and pressure: fast, forgiving and endlessly tweakable."
tags:
  - coffee
  - brewing
---

Grounds steep briefly, then you press the water through a paper filter by hand. That makes it part [[French press]], part [[Espresso]]: the gentle pressure speeds things up, and the paper keeps the cup clean.

It is famous for its recipe culture. Changing [[Grind size]], [[Water temperature]] and steep time gives very different cups, so it is a good tool for learning [[Extraction]].
