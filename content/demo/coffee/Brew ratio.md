---
title: "Brew ratio"
description: "The ratio of coffee to water sets how strong a cup is: around 1:15–1:17 for filter, about 1:2 for espresso."
tags:
  - coffee
---

Strength and [[Extraction]] are different things. Strength is how _concentrated_ the cup is; extraction is how much of the coffee you _dissolved_. A weak cup can be over-extracted, and a strong cup can be under-extracted.

- Filter: 1:15 to 1:17, e.g. 15 g coffee to 240 g water.
- [[Espresso]]: about 1:2, e.g. 18 g in, 36 g out.

Change the ratio to fix strength; change [[Grind size]] to fix extraction. Strength is measured as [[Total dissolved solids]].
