---
title: "Total dissolved solids"
description: "TDS measures how strong a cup is: the share of the drink that is dissolved coffee, typically 1.15–1.45% for filter."
tags:
  - coffee
---

A refractometer reads TDS from a few drops. Combined with the dose and the beverage weight, it gives the extraction yield:

$$\text{extraction} = \frac{\text{TDS} \times \text{beverage mass}}{\text{dose}}$$

That separates the two things taste blurs together: strength ([[Brew ratio]]) and [[Extraction]].
