---
title: "Channeling"
description: "When water finds a weak path through an espresso puck and rushes through it, over-extracting that path while the rest stays under-extracted."
tags:
  - coffee
  - brewing
---

An [[Espresso]] puck should resist water evenly. If the grounds are clumpy or the tamp is uneven, water takes the easiest route. That route over-extracts and the rest under-extracts, so the shot tastes both sour and bitter at once.

Signs: a fast, pale shot, spurts from the portafilter, or holes in the spent puck. Fixes: break up clumps, distribute evenly, tamp level. A good [[Burr grinder]] helps too.
