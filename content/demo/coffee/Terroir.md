---
title: "Terroir"
description: "Altitude, soil, climate and variety give a coffee its origin character, the way they do for wine."
tags:
  - coffee
  - beans
---

Higher altitude means cooler nights and slower ripening, which produces denser beans with more acidity and complexity. That is why speciality labels print the elevation.

Broad strokes: Ethiopian coffees are often floral and tea-like, Kenyan ones blackcurrant-bright, Colombian ones caramel-sweet. [[Processing methods]] and [[Roast level]] can amplify or erase all of it; species sets the outline (see [[Arabica and robusta]]) and [[Varieties]] fill in the detail.
