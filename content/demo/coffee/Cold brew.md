---
title: "Cold brew"
description: "Coffee steeped in cold water for 12–24 hours: low acidity, sweet and smooth, because cold water extracts different compounds."
tags:
  - coffee
  - brewing
---

Cold water dissolves acids and bitter compounds much more slowly than hot water, so a long steep pulls mostly sweetness and body. The result tastes rounder and less bright than hot coffee (see [[Acidity]] and [[Sweetness]]).

Usually brewed as a concentrate at about 1:5 to 1:8, then diluted. A coarse [[Grind size]] keeps filtering easy. Note that cold brew is not iced coffee: iced coffee is hot coffee cooled down.
