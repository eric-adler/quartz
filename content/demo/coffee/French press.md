---
title: "French press"
description: "Coarse grounds steeped in hot water for about four minutes, then pressed: a full-bodied, slightly silty cup."
tags:
  - coffee
  - brewing
---

The simplest immersion brewer: grounds and water sit together the whole time, so [[Extraction]] is even and forgiving. A coarse [[Grind size]] keeps it from going bitter over four minutes.

The metal mesh lets oils and fine particles through, which is why French press tastes heavy and rich compared with [[Pour-over]]. That richness is what tasters call [[Body]].

Tip: let the cup rest a few minutes after pressing so the fines settle.
