title: "Espresso"
description: "Hot water forced through a fine, compacted puck at about 9 bar: concentrated, fast, and unforgiving."

A typical shot: 18 g of finely ground coffee, 36 g of liquid out, in 25–30 seconds. That is a [[Brew ratio]] of 1:2 and an extraction squeezed into half a minute, so small changes in [[Grind size]] show up immediately.

**Channelling** is the classic failure: water finds a crack in the puck and rushes through it, over-extracting that path while the rest stays under-extracted. The foam on top is [[Crema]].
