---
title: "Body"
description: "How heavy coffee feels in the mouth, from watery to syrupy. It comes from oils and fine particles."
tags:
  - coffee
  - taste
---

Body is texture rather than taste. Oils and tiny suspended particles make coffee feel heavier. A [[French press]] has lots of body because its metal filter lets both through; paper filters in [[Pour-over]] remove them for a lighter, cleaner cup.

[[Arabica and robusta|Robusta]] and natural [[Processing methods]] tend to add body. [[Espresso]] has the most, thanks to pressure and emulsified oils.
