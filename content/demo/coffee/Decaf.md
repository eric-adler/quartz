---
title: "Decaf"
description: "Green beans soaked to remove about 97% of their caffeine before roasting. Modern methods keep most of the flavour."
tags:
  - coffee
  - beans
---

Caffeine is removed from the green, unroasted beans. The main methods use water and a solvent, water and carbon filters (Swiss Water), or pressurised CO₂. All of them pull a little flavour out along with the [[Caffeine]].

Decaf beans roast and age differently, so they often taste flat if handled like regular beans. Good decaf from a careful roaster can be hard to tell apart (see [[Roast level]] and [[Freshness]]).
