---
title: "Pour-over"
description: "Filter coffee made by pouring water over grounds in a cone. It gives clarity and control."
tags:
  - coffee
  - brewing
---

Water flows through a bed of medium-fine grounds and a paper filter. The paper holds back oils and fine particles, so the cup is clean and transparent, with less [[Body]] than a [[French press]], good for tasting [[Terroir]].

The steps: [[Bloom]], then pour in slow spirals. Total time is roughly 2:30–3:30 for one cup. Too fast and sour usually means a finer [[Grind size]]; too slow and bitter means coarser.
