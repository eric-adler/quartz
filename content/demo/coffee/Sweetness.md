---
title: "Sweetness"
description: "Caramel, chocolate and fruit sweetness from sugars and browning compounds: the sign of a well-balanced extraction."
tags:

---

Green coffee contains sugars, and roasting turns many of them into caramel and browning compounds. Sweetness dissolves after the acids and before the harsh compounds, so it peaks in the middle of [[Extraction]].

That is why baristas chase sweetness when dialling in. Too little extraction tastes sour, too much tastes bitter, and the sweet spot is between them. Steamed milk adds its own sweetness (see [[Milk and microfoam]]).
