---
title: "Water temperature"
description: "Hotter water extracts faster. Most brewing happens between 90 and 96 °C."
tags:
  - coffee
---

Heat speeds up dissolving, so temperature is a gentler version of the [[Grind size]] dial.

- **Light roasts** are dense and less soluble; they usually want water near boiling.
- **Dark roasts** are porous and extract easily; a few degrees cooler tames [[Bitterness]].

See [[Roast level]] for why the two behave so differently.
