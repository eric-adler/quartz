---
title: "Burr grinder"
description: "Two cutting burrs that crush beans to a consistent size. The single biggest upgrade for home coffee."
tags:
  - coffee
  - brewing
---

A blade grinder smashes beans randomly, producing dust and boulders at the same time. Burrs grind each particle down until it fits through a set gap, so the size is consistent. That consistency is what makes [[Grind size]] a reliable dial for [[Extraction]].

Flat burrs tend to give a very uniform, clear cup; conical burrs are common in hand grinders and give a bit more body. For [[Espresso]] the grinder matters even more than the machine, because uneven grounds cause [[Channeling]].
