---
title: "Grind size"
description: "Finer grounds expose more surface area, so water extracts faster. Grind is the strongest single dial you have."
tags:
  - coffee
---

Halve the size of a particle and you roughly double its surface area per gram. More surface means faster [[Extraction]], so grind size sets the pace for everything else.

- [[Espresso]] needs a fine grind because water passes through in under 30 seconds.
- [[Pour-over]] sits in the middle: medium-fine.
- French press steeps for minutes, so it wants a coarse grind.

**Uniformity matters as much as size.** Blade grinders smash beans into dust (_fines_, which over-extract) and chunks (_boulders_, which under-extract) at the same time, giving a cup that is bitter _and_ sour. Burr grinders cut evenly.
