---
title: "Acidity"
description: "The bright, juicy, sometimes sour side of coffee. Pleasant acidity tastes like fruit, unpleasant acidity like vinegar."
tags:
  - coffee
  - taste
---

Coffee contains citric, malic, phosphoric and other acids. Tasters describe good acidity as _bright_: lemon, apple, berries. It is strongest in light roasts from high-altitude farms (see [[Terroir]] and [[Roast level]]).

Acids dissolve first, so a cup that tastes _sour_ rather than bright is usually under-extracted ([[Extraction]]). [[Water chemistry]] matters too: bicarbonate in hard water mutes acidity. [[Cold brew]] is low in acidity by nature.
