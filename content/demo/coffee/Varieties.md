title: "Varieties"
description: "Within arabica, varieties such as Typica, Bourbon and Gesha differ in flavour, yield and disease resistance, much like grape varieties in wine."
tags:

---

Most arabica descends from a few plants taken out of Ethiopia and Yemen. **Typica** and **Bourbon** are the classic lines; **Gesha**, rediscovered in Panama in the 2000s, became famous for jasmine-like aromatics and record prices.

Newer hybrids trade some cup quality for resistance to leaf rust and higher yields. Variety is one ingredient of [[Terroir]], alongside altitude and [[Processing methods]], and sits within the species split described in [[Arabica and robusta]].
