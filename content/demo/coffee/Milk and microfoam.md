---
title: "Milk and microfoam"
description: "Steamed milk with tiny, glossy bubbles: the texture behind a good flat white and the canvas for latte art."
tags:
  - coffee
  - brewing
---

Steaming does two things: it heats the milk and it injects air. Good microfoam has bubbles too small to see, so the milk looks like wet paint and blends with the [[Espresso]] instead of sitting on top of it.

Heat matters as much as air. Milk tastes sweetest around 55–65 °C; above that, proteins break down and the sweetness fades (see [[Sweetness]]).
