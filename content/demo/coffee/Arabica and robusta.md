---
title: "Arabica and robusta"
description: "The two species behind nearly all coffee: arabica for sweetness and acidity, robusta for body, bitterness and caffeine."
tags:
  - coffee
---

**Arabica** (_Coffea arabica_) grows high and slowly and is fussy about climate. It makes up most speciality coffee.

**Robusta** (_Coffea canephora_) is hardier and grows lower and hotter. It has roughly twice the caffeine and more chlorogenic acids, which taste bitter. It adds body and [[Crema]] to espresso blends.

Species is one layer of [[Terroir]]; variety (Gesha, Bourbon, Typica…) is the next.
