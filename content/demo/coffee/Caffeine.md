---
title: "Caffeine"
description: "The bitter alkaloid that makes coffee a stimulant. Robusta has about twice as much as arabica."
tags:
  - coffee
  - beans
---

Caffeine blocks adenosine, the molecule that makes you feel tired. A typical cup has around 80–120 mg, depending mostly on dose and species: [[Arabica and robusta|robusta]] beans carry roughly twice the caffeine of arabica.

It tastes bitter, but contributes only part of coffee's [[Bitterness]]. Roasting barely changes caffeine content, so a dark roast is not "stronger". [[Decaf]] removes most of it.
