---
title: "Cupping"
description: "The professional tasting protocol: every coffee brewed the same simple way so that only the beans differ."
tags:
  - coffee
  - taste
---

Coarse grounds go into bowls, hot water is poured over them, and after four minutes the crust is broken and skimmed. Tasters slurp from spoons to spray coffee across the palate.

Because the recipe is fixed, differences in [[Acidity]], [[Sweetness]], [[Body]] and [[Bitterness]] come from the coffee itself: its [[Terroir]], [[Varieties]], [[Processing methods]] and [[Roast level]]. Buyers use cupping to choose lots; roasters use it to check every batch.
