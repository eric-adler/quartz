---
title: "Moka pot"
description: "A stovetop brewer that pushes boiling water up through coffee with steam pressure. Strong, but not espresso."
tags:
  - coffee
  - brewing
---

Water in the bottom chamber heats, steam pressure builds (around 1–2 bar, far below [[Espresso]]'s 9) and pushes water up through the grounds into the top chamber.

Because the water is near boiling, moka coffee easily tastes harsh. Take it off the heat as soon as it starts to gurgle, and use a slightly coarser [[Grind size]] than espresso. See [[Water temperature]] and [[Bitterness]].
