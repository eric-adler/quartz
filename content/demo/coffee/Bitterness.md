---
title: "Bitterness"
description: "Bitterness has three usual sources: over-extraction, dark roasting and robusta. Only the first is a brewing problem."
tags:
  - coffee
---

1. **Over-extraction:** the water went on past the sugars into the heavy compounds. Fixed by brewing: coarser [[Grind size]], cooler [[Water temperature]], shorter contact. See [[Extraction]].
2. **Dark roasting:** roast bitterness is part of the coffee you bought. See [[Roast level]].
3. **Robusta:** more chlorogenic acids and caffeine, both bitter. See [[Arabica and robusta]].

Stale coffee is a common suspect but usually tastes _flat_ rather than bitter ([[Freshness]]).
