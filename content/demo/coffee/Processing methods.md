---
title: "Processing methods"
description: "How the fruit is removed from the seed after harvest (washed, natural or honey) changes flavour as much as origin does."
tags:
  - coffee
---

A coffee bean is the seed of a cherry. After picking, the fruit has to come off.

- **Washed:** fruit stripped, seeds fermented in water and dried. Clean, bright, transparent.
- **Natural:** whole cherries dried in the sun, seed removed later. Fruity, heavy, sometimes winey.
- **Honey:** skin removed, sticky fruit left on during drying. In between.

Together with [[Terroir]], processing explains most of what is on a bag's tasting notes.
