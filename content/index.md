#### Credence goods
Search goods are verifiable before purchase. You can sit on a chair and measure it's proportions before you buy it. 
Experience goods are verifiable after. You can tell if the steak was tasty after you ate it. 
Credence goods stay unverifiable regardless because the buyer lacks the technical basis to judge whether the intervention was necessary, sufficient, or correctly executed.
You don't really know if the medical diagnosis or the legal advice was worth it because you lack the expertise you payed for.
